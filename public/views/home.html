How to Play

Draw and identify sketches of assigned topics to earn points. Each round is composed of four phases

  1. Word assignment You will be shown a word to draw. Spend this time reading the word and designing your masterpiece.
  2. Draw your word. You will have a short time to draw your assigned word.
  3. Sort the drawings. Review the drawings of other players and determine if they drew the same thing as you or something else.
  4. Reveal and score. Reveal what every player was drawing and who identified it correctly. Earn points for each drawing you identify correcty and each person who correctly identified your drawing.

Repeat the process for multiple rounds to accumulate points and determine the winner!
